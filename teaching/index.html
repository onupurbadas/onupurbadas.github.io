Teaching

I see the classroom as a space where ideas meet real-world experience: where students compare theory to the realities around them, question the systems they live in, and learn that economics is about decisions that affect lives. My teaching is participatory, inclusive, and grounded in real-world stories, and I aim to make quantitative methods feel approachable to every student.
