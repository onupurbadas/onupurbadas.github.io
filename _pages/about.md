I'm an applied microeconomist studying how **power and structural inequality shape environmental and economic outcomes**. I received my Ph.D. from UMass Amherst in 2026, advised by [Jamie T. Mullins](https://people.umass.edu/jmullins/index.html), and am currently on the job market.

Using India's randomized political reservations in village councils, I study what happens to forests and local economies when women and marginalized caste groups take office. I also study health and public policy: how rising temperatures and air pollution affect the mental health of older adults, and how hospitals can prevent pneumonia among patients. I combine satellite data, nighttime lights, GIS, administrative records, and large surveys.

#### Teaching
I teach economics through curiosity, empathy, and a critical lens. As the Econometrics and Data Science Fellow at [Amherst College](https://www.amherst.edu/academiclife/departments/economics), I supported students learning econometrics and data analysis. At UMass's [Institute for Social Science Research](https://www.umass.edu/social-science-research/), I lead Stata workshops and advise researchers and students on quantitative methods.
