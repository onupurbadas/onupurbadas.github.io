<p>
I see the classroom as a space where ideas meet real-world experience: where students compare theory to the realities around them, question the systems they live in, and learn that economics is about decisions that affect lives. My teaching is <strong>participatory, inclusive, and grounded in real-world stories</strong>, and I aim to make quantitative methods feel approachable to every student.
</p>
